# Giant Panda Maths

The Key Stage 3 member of the Panda Maths family, for 11–14 year olds (Years 7–9). After [Little Panda Maths](https://github.com/pjanak/little-panda-maths) (KS1) and [Big Panda Maths](https://github.com/pjanak/big-panda-maths) (KS2), Bao is now a giant panda, with glasses and headphones. The methods are the ones used in "maths mastery" classrooms, which grew out of the England–Shanghai teacher exchange.

## What's different for teenagers

- **XP, levels and streaks** instead of stars: 10 XP for a first-try answer, 5 XP after a wrong one, and a new level every 100 XP.
- **A method for every question.** A "Show the method" button explains how to solve it before answering; it costs nothing, and it opens by itself after two wrong tries. Wrong answers also get a targeted hint, and correct answers show the worked solution.
- **A number pad with a minus key and decimal point.** A physical keyboard works too (digits, `-`, Backspace, Enter).
- A graph-paper look, and read-aloud off by default.

## Topics

**Number**

- **Negative Numbers (正负数)**: adding and subtracting on a number line, multiplying and dividing, then BIDMAS with negatives.
- **Factors and Primes (短除法)**: primes and HCF, then the Chinese short-division ladder for HCF and LCM, then prime factorisation in index form.
- **Powers and Standard Form (乘方)**: squares, cubes and roots, the index laws, standard form.
- **Ratio and Percentages (比例)**: simplifying and sharing ratios, multipliers, reverse percentages, percentage change, compound interest.

**Algebra and geometry**

- **Solving Equations (方程)**: shown as a balance; two-step equations, unknowns on both sides, brackets and fractions.
- **Expressions (代数)**: substitution, collecting like terms, expanding single and double brackets, factorising quadratics.
- **Sequences (数列)**: term-to-term rules, the nth term, checking membership, quadratic sequences.
- **Angles, Area and Pythagoras (勾股定理)**: angle facts, areas of triangles, parallelograms, trapeziums and circles, then Pythagoras' theorem (the gōu gǔ theorem: 勾三股四弦五, "3, 4, 5").

## Year groups

| Topic | Year 7 | Year 8 | Year 9 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Negative Numbers | Add and subtract | Multiply and divide | BIDMAS with negatives and powers |
| Factors and Primes | Primes, HCF | Ladder for HCF and LCM | Prime factorisation, LCM |
| Powers and Standard Form | Squares, cubes, roots | Index laws | Standard form |
| Ratio and Percentages | Simplify and share | Multipliers, % increase and decrease | Reverse %, % change, compound interest |
| Solving Equations | Two-step | Unknowns on both sides | Brackets, fractions, negative answers |
| Expressions | Substitute, collect like terms | Expand single brackets | Expand double brackets, factorise |
| Sequences | Next term, term-to-term rule | nth term | Using the nth term, quadratic sequences |
| Angles, Area and Pythagoras | Angle facts | Areas, circles | Pythagoras |

Not covered yet: probability, statistics, graphs and transformations.

## Settings

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the home screen for a study timer (15–45 minutes), read-aloud, and resetting XP (with a confirmation step).

## Running it

It's a single static file with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify).
